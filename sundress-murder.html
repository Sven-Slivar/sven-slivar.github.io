Sundress Murder

A mystery horror game. One night the office window tells you your wife isn't who she seems to be, and you work through the cases to find out.

My role

What I built

I owned most of what the player sees and touches. I programmed the game's UI in Unity, then took it through a full visual redesign (SundressUI): one palette, one type scale and one panel style for every screen. I also built the typewriter that talks to the office window, and the office and home levels.

01 · signature mechanic

The typewriter and the window

The window types out what is going to happen, and it only stays a warning if someone answers it. The day you arrive at work to find nobody answered the transcript, the window tells you your wife is not who she seems to be, and from then on you are watching her. I built the typewriter you answer it with, and gave it the same 32 characters-per-second rhythm as the dialogue, so the machine and the story speak with one voice.

32 chars / sec E to inspect objective-driven
02 · dialogue system

A dialogue box that gets out of the way

Typewriter text, numbered answers and a transcript you can scroll back with H. The first version took over half the screen, so I cut it from 600 to 340 px (55% → 31%) by showing one line of history instead of two, without dropping any text below the 24 px minimum. Writer-only tags like "PETR (OFF)" no longer reach the player.

600 → 340 px 24 px text floor hold H: transcript
03 · HUD & menus

One panel style, everywhere

The objectives panel borrows the exact plate the main menu already used (a hairline left border on a translucent fill) instead of inventing a new container, and it sizes to its content. The pause menu got full settings and rebindable controls, including the text reveal speed.

rebindable keys suspicion meter type-only states
04 · level design

Two spaces, one daily loop

The game runs on a day and night cycle, and I built both halves. By day you are at the office: sorting profiles, logging clues in the notebook and typing to the window. By night you go home, explore the house and spend time with a wife you no longer trust. What you learn at work changes what you notice at home, so the two levels pace the reveal together.

Play one day

Three short pieces of the daily loop, rebuilt for the browser: work the case board at the office, answer the window before it comes true, then walk the house at night without waking her.
