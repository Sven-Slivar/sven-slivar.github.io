Johnny, is it still you?

A branching horror story told in second person. You are the voice in Johnny's head as he drives to a rundown motel to kill his wife, and he can't tell you why.

The story

Johnny wakes in a dying car on an empty road, nose bleeding, the gas gauge in the red. In the glove compartment there is a cheap revolver with six rounds. Ahead is a motel at the edge of nowhere, and room 502.

You are the voice he talks to. Through the empty reception, a phone that rings when it shouldn't and a hallway of too many doors, your answers decide what he trusts, and whether the woman waiting behind the door is still the person he married.

Death is not an exit. The story loops, and each time Johnny walks back into the motel knowing a little more.
